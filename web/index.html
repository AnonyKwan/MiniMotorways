How to play

  • Connect colours. Drag from a home to a big building of the same colour and shape. Cars do the rest.
  • Watch the pins. Pins pile up at a building. More than six starts a red countdown; keep cars coming.
  • Spend road tiles wisely. Every road piece costs one tile. Erasing gives it back.
  • Pick an upgrade each week. Bridges cross water, tunnels go through hills, lights and roundabouts tame junctions, motorways jump over everything.

Draw: drag · Erase: right-drag, Ctrl or Tab · Pause: Space · Speed: 1 2 3 · Zoom: wheel or pinch · Pan: middle-drag or two fingers
